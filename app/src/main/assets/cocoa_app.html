100% Offline Privacy Guarantee v1.0

Privacy Policy — Cocoa

1. Zero Cloud Telemetry & 100% Local Execution: Cocoa ("Managing Expenses by Cocoa") operates strictly offline on your Android device / local browser storage. The application requests zero internet permissions in its AndroidManifest and transmits zero personal, banking, or financial data to any external server.

2. Local In-Memory Excel (.xlsx) Processing: When you import or export an Excel workbook, all parsing, normalization, and sheet generation occur exclusively within local device memory.

3. Data Ownership & Sovereign Control: All Monthly Vaults, Fuel logs, Pocket Money micro-spends, and People repayment records remain under your physical control. You may export or permanently wipe your local database at any time using the Reset control above.

Legal Standards Terms

Terms of Use — Managing Expenses by Cocoa

1. Personal Bookkeeping Purpose: Cocoa is provided as a personal expense, sub-budget, and investment tracking utility. Figures, fuel mileage calculations, and SIP tallies are based solely on user-entered or user-imported ledger records.

2. User Responsibility for Local Backups: Because Cocoa stores 100% of data locally on your device without cloud syncing, you are responsible for exporting periodic .xlsx or JSON backups prior to factory-resetting or switching devices.

3. No Financial or Investment Advisory Liability: Portfolio summaries (such as SIP or HSBC mutual fund tracking) are informational bookkeeping views and do not constitute regulated investment advice.
